Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Trinetra Drones & Robotics, Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh

1. Introduction

Trinetra Drones & Robotics ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Trinetra Agriculture Drone Booking App. This Privacy Policy informs farmers, pilots, and visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when using our application and drone spraying services in Andhra Pradesh, India.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, 10-digit Indian mobile number, and Google profile details (when opting for Continue with Google) used strictly for authentication and account security.
  • Agricultural Field Data: Crop type, acreage, village, mandal, district, and field GPS location marker to calculate spraying routes and provide pilot navigation.
  • Booking & Invoicing Records: Drone service selection, service timestamps, DGCA certified pilot assignment, and digital tax invoices.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely to:

  • Send 6-digit one-time password (OTP) verification SMS to your registered mobile number.
  • Dispatch certified DGCA drone pilots to your farm coordinates for precision spraying.
  • Provide live flight telemetry updates (altitude, battery, and sprayed acreage).
  • Generate digital GST and agricultural exemption tax receipts for pay-after-service settlements.

4. Security of Data

We employ industry-standard encryption. Mobile verification codes are stored strictly as irreversible SHA-256 hashes and are never stored in plaintext. We never sell, lease, or share farmer personal data with third-party marketers.
